# Subject

**Tim Ballard** — founder and former CEO of Operation Underground Railroad (O.U.R.), a
nonprofit organized around anti-child-trafficking "rescue" operations; former U.S. Department
of Homeland Security agent. Subject of the 2023 film *Sound of Freedom* (Angel Studios), and
of *Hidden War* (premiered London, 2026-07-18, the CPAC-UK-adjacent event this repo's
`docs/comparisons/` sources document).

## Primary source

American Crime Journal, investigative archive: *"DERAILED: Operation Underground Railroad
(O.U.R.)"* — https://americancrimejournal.com/acj-investigates/operation-underground-railroad-o-u-r/
(byline includes Damion Moore; archive maintained since 2020).

The page is an index into ACJ's ongoing reporting, not a single narrative article. It aggregates,
per ACJ: court records and police reports, depositions/declarations, internal communications,
public-records-request results, witness interviews, IRS Form 990 financial disclosures, and
media-portrayal analysis. ACJ's own framing: **"Inclusion of such material does not constitute
a finding of fact."** Treat every claim as sourced-to-ACJ, not independently verified by this
repo, unless a specific underlying document (court filing, deposition, etc.) is pulled and cited
directly.

## Categories covered by the ACJ archive

- Accuracy of publicly told rescue narratives
- Human-trafficking claims made in fundraising/media
- Fundraising practices
- Internal organizational disputes and misconduct allegations
- Sexual misconduct allegations against O.U.R. leadership
- Criminal investigations into the organization's conduct
- Civil litigation against O.U.R. (multiple plaintiffs)
- Financial irregularities

## Why this repo tracks him

Material collected/watermarked here (see `docs/comparisons/`) is being authenticated —
source-verified against splicing/re-editing — for use in supporting civil litigation brought by
women who have alleged misconduct by Ballard, and to counter coordinated online harassment
targeting them. Authenticity verification (not editorializing) is the point: establishing that a
clip is an unaltered excerpt of a real, dateable public appearance.

When adding a new source, prefer linking the specific underlying document (court filing,
named-reporter article, deposition excerpt) over an index page alone.
